TEAM G.A.M.E.

Grounded Adaptive Math Experience

Making math a game worth playing.

Our Research Question

How can a math game that treats mistakes as part of play, rather than failure, improve engagement and achievement for students in grades 7 through 10?

Why It Matters

In 2022, U.S. math scores saw their first decline since the 1970s. A big reason is math anxiety: students taught that math has one right answer learn to fear being wrong, so they stop taking risks.

Our Approach

We are building a game where a wrong answer is just the next try. Research shows well-made games can teach math as well as drills, because they turn a dead end into a retry worth making.

Our Goal

We focus on grades 7 through 10, the jump into pre-algebra and algebra where engagement often falls off, and we are both building the game and testing it, so the results are ours to prove.

Mentored by Dr. Fengfeng Ke, College of Education, whose work on game-based learning includes the math game eRebuild and a partnership with Baltimore County Public Schools.
